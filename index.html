Act II
Resonance

I think, in a way, we were always sorta connected. Like there has always been a part of me in you, that you have always been with me. It’s only through truly speaking to someone that you realize how few differences there actually are, how thin the line is between you and them.

In some ways I truly do look up to you.

I know you’re my equal and don’t wanna make it seem like anyone’s more superior than the other (although you mog tf outta me) but I think that’s also the beauty of human connection.

Act III
Eudaimonia

We talked about this once—virtue ethics. It’s a philosophy that definitely has its weak points. When I was first learning about it, I thought, well, that isn't great; you’ll always end up idolizing someone and then be disappointed when they aren't who you thought they were.

Through you, though, I think I’ve started to adapt the theory. I don't expect you to be anyone other than exactly who you’ve shown me to be—the person I've had the absolute privilege of getting to know.

Maybe the goal really is just Eudaimonia.

Maybe Aristotle (A RACIST I KNOW) and Plato knew a thing or two after all. Maybe the goal really is just Eudaimonia, and maybe you’re the clearest and most peaceful path I’ve found to get there. I say peaceful because you truly do bring me peace.

Act IV
Ataraxia
Ataraxia: (n.) A lucid state of robust equanimity; an ongoing freedom from distress or worry. Unconditional tranquility.

When you’re around, or when we’re just speaking, I feel so at ease. Your presence alone is enough to make my body completely relax—not in *that* way, you FREAK.

They say that when you’re truly comfortable with someone, you start to drift off, and I’m beginning to think that’s true. People might say it’s because we’re on the phone at six in the morning, but it’s more than that. It’s an inner battle: one half of me is completely at peace and ready to sleep, and the other half is just too excited to be speaking with you to close my eyes.

I used to love going to sleep; I looked forward to it all day long. Now, I only look forward to the night because that’s when my day actually starts. The moon’s out, but it’s the sun that I see.

I could talk about you for hours—I’m honestly surprised my friends haven’t gotten sick of me yet. I physically have to stop myself from bringing you up at every turn, or whenever something reminds me of you (which, for the record, is pretty damn often). You’re lucky I had to confine my thoughts to a single website; otherwise, this could’ve taken every single one of your cookies (👅).

You are truly good at everything, which is just fucking unbelievable. You’re so smart and capable, and your mind works in such beautiful ways. The way you smile makes my heart flutter like a non-creepy butterfly (a rarity). I love your pretty rosy cheeks that flush whenever I look at you, your adorable little laugh, and how you claim to be a pro at eye contact, but really, you’re just a shy baby looking for anything else to focus on. Don't. Focus on me, baby. I got you.
