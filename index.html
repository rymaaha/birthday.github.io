Act I
The Way We Met

Dear Karime,

You are the kindest, most genuine person I have ever met. Honestly, you're so beautiful and I don't really comprehend how it's possible for someone to look the way you do, to feel the way you do. You're so emotionally mature and act so much older than your age (...miller's girl? 😧).

The day before I met you, I was talking about how unbelievable it was that people could meet through pure chance. Then you happened.

Act II
The Line Between Us

I think in a way we were always sorta connected. That there has always been a part of me in you, that you have always been with me.

It's only through speaking to people that you truly understand how thin the differences are, how thin the line is between you and them. That line has been blurred since I've met you.

Not in an omg I'm losing myself and not independent kind of way. More in an oh my god this girl is so much of what I want in myself.

In some ways, I truly do look up to you.

I know you're my equal and don't wanna make it seem like anyone's more superior than the other (although you mog tf outta me), but I think that's also the beauty of human connection.

Act III
Eudaimonia

We spoke about virtue ethics, a philosophy that definitely has its weak points. When I was learning about it I thought, well that isn't great, you'll always end up idolizing someone and then be disappointed when they're not who you thought they are.

Through you, though, I think I may have adapted parts of the theory. Not expecting you to be anyone apart from who you've shown me to be, who I've had the privilege of getting to know.

Maybe Aristotle and Plato knew a thing or two. Maybe the goal really is Eudaimonia.

"Books, she has found, are a way to live a thousand lives—or to find strength in a very long one."
Maybe you're the clearest and most peaceful path I've seen to get there.

I say peaceful because you truly do bring me peace.

Act IV
The Peace You Bring Me

When you're around, when I'm speaking to you, I feel so much more at ease. Like just your presence is enough for my body to relax — not in that way you FREAK.

It's said that when you feel completely comfortable with someone you start to fall asleep, and that's partly true.

You can say it's because we're on the phone at six in the fucking morning, but no. It's that I feel an inner battle going on.

The moon's out, but it's the sun that I see.

I used to love going to sleep, looked forward to it all day. Now I just look forward to the night because that's when my day starts.

Act V
The Thing I Always Mean To Say

I can talk about you for hours. Surprised my friends haven't gotten sick of me yet. I physically have to hold back from talking about you, from bringing you up at any given moment.

You're truly good at everything which is fucking unbelievable. You're so smart and capable. Your mind works in such beautiful ways.

The way you smile makes my heart flutter like a non-creepy butterfly. Your pretty rosy cheeks that get redder when I look at you, your adorable little laugh, and how you claim to be able to hold eye contact but really you're just a shy baby looking around for anything else to focus on.

Don't. Focus on me baby, I got you.
