The Playlist

All your songs

Pretty girl like you, I’m sure you’ve had many playlists made for you, but this one’s a little different. These aren’t exactly songs that remind me of you, or that I think about you when I hear. These are songs you know and love: the ones we’ve talked about, or the ones you’ve sent me a thousand voice notes of, singing in your dorm with Ariana.

They’re the songs you listened to in the car with your brother, the ones you mention in passing, and the ones you sing under your breath when you think no one’s listening or paying attention.

This isn’t a playlist I made for you baby,
it’s one you made for yourself.
