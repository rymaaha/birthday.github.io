When you're hungry

Go eat. Seriously, stop scrolling, stop working, and go get a snack. At least half of the world's problems are just a disguised drop in blood sugar. Get a little treat, you deserve it, and I promise whatever is annoying you will seem 50% less annoying after a good meal.
