The Texas Question

a curated shortlist based on the coordinates: near the border, roughly 8 hours from San Antonio, and 11 hours from El Paso. this is my educated attempt at cracking the case, mind you, keeping in mind that this university must also offer a bachelor's in forensic science, as that is what you are studying.

The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley

Brownsville & Edinburg, TX · Public University
Forensic Science Target Distance Border Proximity

Located right in the valley near the border. If we are calculating distances and specific coordinates, this hits the mark perfectly, and their science programs back it up.

The University of Texas at El Paso

El Paso, TX · Public Research University
Forensic Science Track Far West Mountains

The anchor of West Texas. Famous for its stunning Bhutanese architecture and nestled right into the mountains along the border, with strong investigative science tracks.

Texas A&M International University

Laredo, TX · Public University
Criminal Justice Close-Knit

Another fantastic option deep in South Texas. Smaller, tight-knit, and sits exactly on the cultural crossroads of the border.

UT Austin

Austin, TX · Public Research University
The Classic Science Hub

We can't have a Texas map without it. Regardless of the exact coordinates, Austin's research facilities are unmatched. Hook 'em.
